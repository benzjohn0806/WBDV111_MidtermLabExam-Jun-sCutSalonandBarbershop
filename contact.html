Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Do I need to book an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but we highly recommend booking an appointment to avoid long wait times and guarantee your preferred time slot.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, GCash, Maya, and credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard).

Do you offer services for kids?

Yes! We offer kids haircuts for children 12 years and under for only ₱250.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please notify us at least 2 hours before your appointment for cancellations or rescheduling.

Do you offer bridal packages?

Yes! We offer special bridal packages including hair, makeup, and nail services. Please contact us for more details.
